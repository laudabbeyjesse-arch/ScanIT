About ScanIT

ScanIT is a student‑driven project built at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST), Department of Computer Science, Level 200. Our mission is to make shopping smarter by giving users instant access to product details through barcode scanning and recognition technology.

We believe in combining innovation with simplicity. ScanIT is designed to be lightweight, fast, and user‑friendly, so anyone can scan and learn about products in seconds. This project reflects our passion for technology, problem‑solving, and creating tools that improve everyday life.

Mission

To empower consumers with instant product knowledge.

Vision

To become the go‑to mobile tool for smarter shopping worldwide.

Values

Innovation, simplicity, and accessibility for all users.
